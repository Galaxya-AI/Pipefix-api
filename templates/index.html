PipeFix

Diagnoses failed GitLab pipelines and proposes reviewed fixes

Why did the pipeline fail?

PipeFix reads a failed GitLab pipeline, finds the cause and proposes a fix reviewed by a second agent. Nothing changes in your project until you choose to create the merge request.

This proof of concept analyses one pipeline at a time. Give its number, or leave the field empty to analyse the latest failed pipeline of the project.
